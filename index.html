Lead-in

Today vs yesterday

Answer aloud. Don’t study a rule yet.

TODAY

Are you tired today?

Are you at home now?

Is the weather good?

Is your motorcycle clean?

Are you busy?

YESTERDAY

Were you tired yesterday?

Were you at home?

Was the weather good?

Was your motorcycle clean?

Were you busy?

What changes?

02

Read & listen

Yesterday

Listen first. Open the script only when you’re ready.

Show story script

Yesterday was a busy day.

In the morning, I was at home. I was tired because it was very early.

At 7:00, I was in my car. The roads were busy.

At lunchtime, I was hungry, but my lunch was very simple.

In the evening, I was at home again. My motorcycle was in the garage.

My friends were busy, so I was alone.

At 11:00, I was very tired and I went to bed.

Check understanding

Mini-dialogues

Show dialogue scripts

A: Were you tired yesterday?

A: Why?

A: Was the road busy?
B: Yes, it was.
A: What time?
B: At about 8:00.

A: Were you at home last night?
B: No, I wasn’t.
A: Where were you?
