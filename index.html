One busy day

7:0012:3019:0023:00

Yesterday was a busy day. In the morning, I was at home. I was tired because it was very early.

At 7:00, I was in my car. The roads were busy. At lunchtime, I was hungry, but my lunch was very simple.

In the evening, I was at home again. My motorcycle was in the garage. My friends were busy, so I was alone.
