Tense review

General or happening now?

Choose general or now.

GENERAL

Facts and usual situations

Use the Present Simple for things that are generally true or usually happen.

EXAMPLE

Halloween parties are exciting.

Think: always · usually · every year

or
NOW

Actions happening at this moment

Use the Present Continuous: am / is / are + verb-ing.

EXAMPLE

The boy is carving a pumpkin.

Think: now · at the moment · in this picture

Ask yourself: Is it generally true, or can I see it happening right now?
